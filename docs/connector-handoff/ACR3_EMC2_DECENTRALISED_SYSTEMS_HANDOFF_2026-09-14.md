## Architecture

## Governance

The intended model is staged dual governance:

- **Bootstrap:** Römer + EMASSC retain strategic, legal, safety, IP and technical authority; community mechanisms remain sandboxed/non-critical.
- **Bounded participation:** after legal/security gates, qualified participants may vote only on explicitly delegated ecosystem/lifecycle matters.
- **Progressive decentralisation:** additional powers require mature treasury limits, emergency controls, dispute resolution and compliance responsibilities.

Capped/quadratic voting and guardian/multisig controls are **design mechanisms**, not live governance. Asset/twin participation does not confer company directorship, technical approval, ownership or title without a separate legal instrument.

## Evidence and oracle rule

Material economic state changes follow:

`SOURCE -> CONFIGURATION -> RUN/EVENT -> RAW/PRIMARY EVIDENCE -> DERIVED RESULT -> REVIEW/APPROVAL -> REGISTRY STATE -> OPTIONAL ECONOMIC ACTION`

Oracles must fail closed. Missing evidence must never default to zero, PASS, owned, compliant, transferable, verified or valuable.
