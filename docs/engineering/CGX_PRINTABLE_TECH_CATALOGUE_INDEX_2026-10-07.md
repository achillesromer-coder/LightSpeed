## Assembly/use interpretation

**L1–L2** should be the first public/DIY surface: passive structures, simple conductors/resistors/heaters, basic sensors, benign coatings, identity tags and calibration artefacts where materials/voltages/processes are safe.  
**L3** introduces calibrated mechanisms, fluidics, richer sensors/comms and hybrid assembly.  
**L4** is professional/specialist territory: batteries, supercapacitors, motors, active RF, advanced actuators, lab/process modules and coupled energy structures.  
**L5** covers specialist compute/device fabrication, integrated Mark/Luke/UTP/outpost systems and recursive process-cell expansion.

The AI helper should therefore choose a route by **capability + evidence + hazard + user permission**, not by the word “DIY” alone.

10. **CGX convergence:** PT-071–072, PT-076, PT-079–080 turn the catalogue into a programmable, evidence-aware network rather than a static parts list.

## Held interpretation notes

- **PT-040…042** are printed active-compute research candidates. They do not imply that a generic UTP can print modern smartphone-class semiconductor compute.
- **PT-043** remains the practical hybrid route where dense compute/security is needed.
- **PT-055** legacy wording includes “self-reproduction”; PT-081 now supplies the stricter interpretation: staged child-module manufacture with separate calibration and qualification, never unconstrained autonomous replication.
- **PT-073** explicitly rejects free-space-as-energy-source interpretations.
- **PT-074** requires contamination and application-specific qualification before recovered material becomes feed.
- **PT-075** permits geospatial sensing/control research but not unvalidated biological/resonant efficacy claims.
- **PT-077** captures the three-Mark-III + Mark-V registry/telemetry/tagging arrangement as a founder scenario; Type 1 Systems Solar Hull UC-005 corroborates Mark V registry/telemetry casing and low-power support at design stage, with no ownership/yield claim.
- **PT-078** is tag/track/hazard-envelope infrastructure; deorbit/deflection remains a separately authorised orbital-dynamics problem.
- The legacy catalogue column-N/header semantic mismatch remains a held schema-migration item; this derived view does not reinterpret that column.

## AI-helper minimum query contract

For any requested build, return:

1. requested function and target environment;
2. candidate PT records and why they apply;
3. required feeds/material passports and seed dependencies;
4. required process cells and environment envelopes;
5. compute route: local, delegated, or specialist;
6. DIY/professional permission ceiling;
7. current evidence tier and explicit unknowns;
8. safety/ecology/authority hard gates;
9. assembly sequence and checkpoints;
10. verification tests, expected failure modes and reject path;
11. snapshot/hash/handshake/readback/DBR lineage;
12. safe fallback and recovery/recycling route.

This keeps “print any technology” as a constrained engineering compiler problem rather than an unsupported universal-manufacturing claim.
