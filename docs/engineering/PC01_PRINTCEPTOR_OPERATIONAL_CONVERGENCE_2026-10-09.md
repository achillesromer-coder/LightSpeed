## Interaction grammar

A user/operator interaction resolves first to one of seven intents:

`LOG | BUILD | ENROL | REPAIR | REVIEW | MAINTAIN | QUERY`

This keeps simple work simple. “We made one diode today” can be an append-only quantity event with no false claim of electrical qualification. “Create a robotic print and attach a new .cgx identity” resolves to an exact build packet, assembly DAG, safety envelope, child-component lineage, calibration/test plan, object enrolment and DBR path.

## Filespace / dataspace / physical plane

Filespace owns stable meaning: identity, family tree, configuration, geometry/BOM/BOP, recipes, material passports, evidence, authority, render/sequence specifications and supersession.

Dataspace owns live state and events: queue/task state, leases, telemetry, production counts, solver jobs, review decisions, maintenance state, readbacks, receipts and DBR deltas.

The physical plane contains the actual materials, interfaces, cavities, seed components, controllers, sensors/actuators, service boundaries and machine/tool envelope.

## Compute suitability

C0 deterministic control stays local wherever safety/latency requires it. C1 edge compute is optional. C2 planning/simulation/retrieval can be delegated under an authenticated lease without moving semantic or execution authority.

Low-capability/child-oriented devices default to minimum deterministic logic and identity. PC01 does not place an unrestricted agent/LLM in a child's toy or ordinary appliance. Industrial machines can carry richer local/edge compute only while independent safety remains separate.

No sentience claim is made or required.

## Family tree

A .cgx family is explicit lineage, not autonomous agency. Printer, lot, object, subassembly, variant and successor relations are durable and reviewable. Evidence, authority and capability do not inherit merely because objects are related.

## Maintenance and bounded learning

Idle windows may be used for diagnostics, compatibility checks, simulations, maintenance proposals and bounded process-learning candidates. Silent software, firmware, model or hardware upgrades are prohibited. Changes require rollback and post-change verification.

## Digital review path

PC01 digital review is:

source intake → volumetric twin → slices/sections → conventional baseline solve → bounded Raphael comparison → manufacturing/process checks → high-fidelity render set → owner/engineering review → freeze/return.

The render set should include external, section, exploded and build/assembly-sequence views with unresolved geometry and uncertainty visible.

## Readiness

Current PC01 state is `PLANNED / NOT_BUILT`.

`BUILD_READY` requires independent closure of source, geometry, material, process, tool, metrology, simulation, safety, compute, authority and service dimensions on one frozen configuration. It is never a synonym for BUILT, MEASURED, RELEASED or CERTIFIED.
