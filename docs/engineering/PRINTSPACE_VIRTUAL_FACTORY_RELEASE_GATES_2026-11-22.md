# CGX PrintSpace virtual factory — printer/assembly viewer and operating gate contract

**State (22 November 2026):** offline **digital design demonstrator**, not manufacturer-certified CAD, a multi-material manufacturing solution, a verified working diode/PCB/phone, a real printer, live computer vision, nor an operational flight/terrestrial machine. Builds upon the single `cgx.manufacturing` family on LightSpeed PR #236; no competing root, compiler, agent, LOAD root or DBR service is introduced.

## One hierarchy across the canonical family

**Source and rights → Raphael/CGX geometric references → source-owned part geometry → source-class material planning → parent/child assembly references → printer-family capability → environment/process assumptions → directed process timeline → measured material/tool/electrical witness evidence → C0 and native DBR release → machine execution.** Earlier stages may be digitally displayed without inheriting authorisation from their parents. The existing 411 owner-matrix rows, VGK-40 and T173 admission rules remain authoritative only in their own checked scope; T174 is reserved. An H0 content reference is not a real LOAD hydrated object.

This milestone introduces **one bounded catalogue** (`cgx/manufacturing/printspace_virtual_factory_catalog_v0_1.json`), **one deterministic virtual-factory scene emulator** (`virtual_factory.py`) and **one offline, third-party viewer** (`printspace_studio_v0_1.html`). The HTML file is generated reproducibly from the catalogue and a single build template. It is pure offline Canvas and standard browser JS: no WebGL dependency, backend, licensed assets, cloud telemetry or native tool control. Parts are deliberately rectangular visual blocks; conceptual printhead animation is a display state, not G-code.

Seven printer-family roles are taken from the existing PC01 operational route family: structural, functional, fibre, hybrid, feedstock, metrology and mobile. The illustrative machine envelope numbers in the simulator are **invented tutorial dimensions** and must not be interpreted as actual printer design specifications. The user can independently scale the illustrative machine and the product to study model-bed fit, and orbit or zoom the scene. Environment selection covers a terrestrial workshop, controlled enclosure, and a representative remote/in-situ vacuum/low-gravity scenario. These are planning labels; real vacuum, dusty surfaces, radiation, fluid flow, heat transfer, pressure seals, safety and process calibration are **not simulated or certified**.

## Device hierarchy and limits

| Product demonstration | Subsystems depicted | Critical caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Single diode/interlayer | Three-region PC01 synthetic structure, interlayer and conductor; external terminal concept | A conductive/dielectric stack is not proof of a semiconductor junction or a working diode |
| Board/sensor module | Board substrate, conceptual traces, child diode block, third-party IC and connector | This is not a verified PCB stack-up, reflow process, circuit schematic or functioning sensor |
| Phone-scale assembly | Chassis, child board, processor, battery, RF, camera, display and cover | Most critical subsystems are `SOURCE_REQUIRED`, and this is not an entirely printed, electrically functional or approved handset |

Conceptual components use reusable source IDs and hierarchical parent references, not duplicated validated fabrication methods. New customer applications can add bounded products and parts to the catalogue with explicit provenance/evidence flags. No interface can assert product functionality solely from an attached visual geometry or source-class alias.

## Repeatable operations and sweeps

The scene compiler is a deterministic pure computation over the canonical demonstrator catalogue. It binds product, selected printer-family role, environment profile, **product scale**, **printer scale**, and progress within a bounded operation timeline. Each scene contains an input-derived SHA-256, exact visible part-state, illustrative bed fit and explicit unverified operational flags. `reverse_scene` recomputes an original catalogue and selection and requires full equality, rejecting tampered render/assembly output. It does **not** authenticate external physical source bytes, source CAD, machine firmware or actual materials.

The bounded full factorial example is **3 product types × 7 printer-family projections × 3 environment profiles × 4 product scales × 3 hypothetical printer scales = 756 cases**. That is a categorical/size *screen*, not a real process simulation or 756 verified prints. It complements rather than duplicates the existing separately source-bound **216-case six-variable electrothermal/thermoelastic surrogate**, which does not model the actual whole phone, source-verified inter-material chemistry, in-situ process dynamics, semiconductor operation or 4D phase activation. Cross-domain numerical findings are not inherited by larger assemblies without separately validated models and scale-transfer constraints.

The offline viewer supports rotate/orbit, perspective, scene zoom, product/printer independent scale, family/environment selection, exploded subassembly view, clickable parts, playback and export of a **non-authoritative read-only scene JSON**. It marks third-party components and unverified stages. It is not a full CAD viewer; none of the renders are substituted for a source-licensed part mesh. Unsupported machine/model combinations show synthetic envelope holds instead of secretly rescaling the part to fit.

## Reverse regression and operating acceptance

Regression should include: product/child identity and acyclicity; nonnegative bounded coordinates and orientations; no out-of-envelope geometry; no implicit external-part manufacture; stable SHA output; 756-case bounded enumeration and source identity; replay after source mutation; independence of printer/product scaling; explicit in-situ unqualified status; all sequence phases non-actuating; rejected NaNs, mutated source rights, unsafe authorisation claims and invalid scale. Browser QA includes offline load, actual rendered canvas, product change, printer/environment change, product and machine scaling, stage stepping, mobile responsive and export JSON. The separate 21-stage PrintSpace manufacturing/cross-sweep suite remains canonical, extended to **22 sequential stages** with these virtual-factory tests.

Before **hardware operation or productisation**, the owner must supply and independently verify: actual mechanical CAD/kinematic/dimensional printer drawings, tool calibration and resolution envelopes; a valid device-safety case and C0 motion permission; real material-lot passports and directional compatibility; measured terrestrial and in-situ thermochemical and environmental process data; deposition sequencing, atmospheres, pressure isolation, tool-change offsets and encapsulation protocols; qualified electrical circuits, external component procurement and assembly; raw inspection/functional test receipts and uncertainty; rights/licences and DBR admission; repeatability and service/support/maintenance economics. The first empirical gate remains **PC01 bidirectional interface coupons on calibrated hardware**—not a phone printing demonstration.

**Commercialisation path:** virtual factory educational/read-only product can be separately considered for external release only after source/licence/privacy and safety copy review; services for verified devices require distinct empirical claims, provenance, process liability controls, operator training and post-sale support. No material, electronics, or in-situ facility has been physically commissioned by this milestone.

**Achilles pre-DBR:** preserve existing 30 protected source/evidence references and three historical reversible archive candidates, extend exact protected pointers for these modules, and do not archive/delete originals without independent source and recovery receipts. The RDC 25% normal reserve/24% emergency floor remains untouched. Any Github Actions success is a software review receipt, not native owner/operator approval.
