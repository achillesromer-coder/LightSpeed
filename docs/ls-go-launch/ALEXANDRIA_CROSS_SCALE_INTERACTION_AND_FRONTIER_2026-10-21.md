## Resolution

The useful shared abstraction is not that a planet, relay, robot, battery, asteroid and one information bit are physically equivalent. It is that many such systems can be **described** using shared, typed mathematical structures: inventories; conservation; sources/sinks; forces/fluxes; fields; interfaces; transfer functions; geometry; time evolution; controls; rights; provenance and uncertainty. Their units and legal/physical conditions remain different. A joule is energy; a bit represents information. A source-graph connection does not turn the latter into the former or validate a novel power mechanism.

We have attached seven source-graded, read-only **operator-family links** to the existing Raphael geometry atlas (`RGS-000` conventional baseline), existing CGX assurance-method registry and the existing PrintSpace horizon/solid-state contract. None replaces the parent CGX/CCC registry, GMAT, MPL, the existing Alexandria source library or proprietary R0 source.

1. **Conservation and transport:** distinguish material/energy inventory from environmental disposal and ecologically acceptable handling. A detected fragment is not debris remediated.
2. **Constitutive law and fluid regimes:** shared Navier–Stokes conservation *families*, with viscosity `mu` (Pa·s), `nu=mu/rho` (m²/s), Reynolds `Re=rho U L/mu`; prohibit applying one Newtonian continuum fit to a non-Newtonian resin, compressible atmospheric boundary layer or free-molecular regime.
3. **Geometry, dynamics and transfer:** refer each position/velocity to an epoch and coordinate frame; inertial orbital `specific energy = v²/2 - GM/r` is only a two-body diagnostic, not proof of safe binding, debris cleanup or ice absorption. A counterfactual orbit requires supplied momentum/energy, perturbations and collision probability, not "ahead of orbit" geometry alone.
4. **Electromagnetics and observation:** a calibrated passive receiver captures naturally arriving signal; active radar transmits and receives response. Spectroscopy/backscatter require measured spectrum, wavelength, beam shape, antenna effective area, noise, path loss and scattering coefficients. Reflections and harmonic/phase cancellation do not globally recapture all outbound radiation or generate energy. The Sun radiates a broad EM spectrum; Schumann resonances are not a replacement for broadband authenticated communications.
5. **Multi-phase interfaces, capture and gradients:** ice deposition, dusty regolith concentration, printed microstructure, water contamination and material purification are separate constitutive/boundary problems. Track fate as `input = recovered + stored + emitted + unknown`; a mass balance does not certify a safe ecology.
6. **Information/source/semantic transfer:** Object_ID, original SHA, owner/ACL, cross-domain typed link, time/frame and rights stay first-class. GenUI can display the same source-qualified object through maps, 3D, source/kit, optical/spectral, orbit or PrintSpace views; it cannot promote an unsupported inference by visual appeal.
7. **Assurance and owner-gated execution:** original source, falsifier and independent physical or native testing precede DBR admission. Source rights and Australian SAI opt-in are separate from geographical location or commercial bidding.
