2. **EM propagation, spectroscopy and passive observation:** reuse known field/scattering forms. Passive receiver functionality is **not** evidence of harvested power or universal sensing.
3. **Orbital material transfer:** a prospective material release or debris transfer must independently solve relative trajectories, epochs, momentum exchange, capture energy dissipation, collision/fragmentation, contamination and ownership. 4. **Energy accounting:** RF/solar/thermal harvesting and counter-phase field shaping require explicit input, useful stored output, reradiation and dissipative losses. Destructive interference at one receiver does not annul emitted energy everywhere or demonstrate net free energy.
