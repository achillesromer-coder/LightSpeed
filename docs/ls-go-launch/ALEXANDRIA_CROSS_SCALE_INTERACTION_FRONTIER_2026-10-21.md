## Six common classes

1. `TR-01` transport with density, viscosity/rheology and Reynolds/flow regime. Water/air/resin/aqueous contaminants/propellants all need different material equations, thermal response, free surfaces, phases and boundary conditions. VGK-019/020/039 and CIM-026 are reused. Viscosity is a constitutive parameter; aerodynamics is a fluid mechanics domain, not an alternative conservation law.
2. `TR-02` passive EM scattering/receiving and intercepted energy. VGK-037/CIM-009/CIM-043 already demand the incident field, effective aperture, calibrated ports and Poynting balance. Radiation travelling away into space cannot be assumed recoverable system-wide by a counterwave. A reflectometer or radiometer measures received field; a rectenna converts only physically incident collected power, at finite efficiency. No ambient power or RF network capacity is inferred.
3. `TR-03` acoustic sonar and EM passive/active remote sensing. VGK-038/CIM-031 share observations and wave/echo topology but **not** one propagation medium or source budget. Schumann resonance is Earth–ionosphere ELF field structure, not proof of planet-scale broadband networking or a usable power supply.
4. `TR-04` debris, regolith and proposed body/deposition operations. Tracking and shifting an asteroid particle may share mass/momentum bookkeeping with a printer feedstock or logistics node, but must account for orbital state, change of velocity, collision probability, impact energy, thermal/volatile loss, dispersal, chemistry and environmental restrictions. Proposed small dense ice deposition or artificial celestial body accumulation remains an explicitly open theoretical mission/planetary-science Frontier object, not a practical validated automatic gravitational absorption process. NASA's published debris mitigation requirements and planetary-protection obligations are review references, not CGX certification.
5. `TR-05` 4D printed component, modular node, capsule and interplanetary supply node similarity. The shared pattern is a *typed topology* of interface, port, geometry, swept volume, material sequence and custody—not identity of a bit, joule, particle, device or planet. Do not add physically incompatible quantities as if conserved.
6. `TR-06` conservation/transport residual and loss logs for each domain. Mass [kg], momentum [kg·m/s], energy [J], charge [C] and information [bit] are different dimensional quantities. Preserve measurement uncertainties, sinks, source authority and positive residual until actually accounted for.

## Tested synthetic baseline, not a physical result
- Ideal circular tube Hagen–Poiseuille for steady, fully developed, incompressible Newtonian laminar flow: numeric only when attributable geometry/material/Re and the qualified regime are supplied; laminar bound used conservatively.
- Passive captured-power bound: `P_delivered ≤ irradiance × effective_aperture × coupling_efficiency × converter_efficiency`, both efficiencies within [0,1]. An effective aperture and incident irradiance must refer to the same frequency, wave polarization, spatial field and matching conditions; do not treat this as a universal collecting area under all conditions.
- Encounter relative kinetic energy `0.5 × reduced_mass × relative_speed²`; this is not orbit capture, accretion, safe deorbit or ecosystem construction. Loss of this relative energy through dissipation, propulsion or other mechanisms requires separate proof.
- Power accounting `incident = useful + thermal_loss + stored + residual`; residual need not be zero until observed fluxes and uncertainty close. No efficiency or energy creation claim.
- Tests refuse missing source Git identities, unknown VGK/CIM codes, invalid/nonnumeric material parameters, turbulent reduced equations, greater-than-unity energy capture, output greater than input, silent public or native promotion and bit↔joule dimensional conflation.
