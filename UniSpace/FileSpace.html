How to open a carrier
  1. Keep CGX.cgx with this tutorial. If you downloaded the kit, extract its folder and open FileSpace.html in a browser.
  2. Select the carrier below. The reader checks its identity and opens the contents index locally. No account is required.
  3. Choose a geometric quadrant, then select a file to inspect its cell. Export the original bytes, or export the matching files as a source-linked JSON series.
