02 — THE SCIENCE

What we know.
What remains open.

A rigorous digital artefact must preserve the distinction between known physical properties, interpretations of limited observations and outstanding measurement questions.

01 / BODY

A stone from
another epoch.

Apophis is classified as an S-type / stony near-Earth asteroid. Its estimated mean diameter is roughly 340 m. Radar suggests an elongated or potentially bilobed shape; detailed terrain remains unmeasured.

NASA physical overview ↗
02 / DYNAMICS

An orbit changed
by gravity.

Earth's 2029 encounter will alter Apophis' heliocentric orbit. Tidal forces may change its rotation or surface. The drawing and surface rendering on this page do not claim to resolve those changes.

JPL observing plan ↗
03 / DEFENCE

A safe passage.
A rare laboratory.

NASA rules out an Earth impact for at least 100 years. Apophis remains scientifically important as an opportunity to validate models of close gravitational encounters and natural hazards.
