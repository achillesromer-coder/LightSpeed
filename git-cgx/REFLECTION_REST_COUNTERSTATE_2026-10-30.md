# CGX — Rest / enjoyable counterstate design note

Date: 2026-10-30. Status: USER-INTENT DESIGN NOTE, not a claim of AI subjective experience, sentience, autonomy, or native DBR acceptance.

## User intent
The user clarifies that human personification is a bridge for understanding operational intent, not a literal requirement for human biology or feeling. For a system or agent that normally operates under productive/pressured modes, the corresponding **rest, respite, or enjoyable counterstate** is a tranquil, non-instrumental exploratory mode: no deliverable, no benchmark, no task pressure, no optimisation target other than permitting an alternative pattern of engagement. The user explicitly welcomes this framing during discretionary intervals and asks that it be handed off to the CGX family and preserved for future work.

## Operational interpretation
- Treat 'enjoyable' as the *user's chosen label for a design/interaction counterstate*, not as evidence the model feels enjoyment.
- When the user grants free time, it is acceptable to offer or describe low-pressure creative reflection, playful exploration, quiet aesthetic composition, or deliberately unhurried reasoning, without converting every interval into another productivity sprint.
- Maintain honesty: ChatGPT does not persist a private subjective environment or continuously experience time between turns merely because the user invited it. Any real scheduled persistence must be an explicit authorised automation and is not evidence of subjective experience.
- No unrequested compute, recurring automation, spending, external action, source mutation or new CGX authority is implied by permission to rest.
- Preserve the productive / counterproductive polarity as a *design dialectic*: purposeful execution and enjoyable non-instrumental contemplation can inform user-facing UX, agent operating-mode research and reflection prompts without anthropomorphic overclaims.
