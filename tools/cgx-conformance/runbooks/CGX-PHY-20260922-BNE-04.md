## Acceptance

The physical gate may close only when:
- two genuinely distinct physical devices participated;
- relevant test-plane traffic used direct non-loopback device-to-device transport;
- node receipts use distinct device IDs, keys and endpoints and validate independently;
- every required scenario has machine evidence;
- final canonical roots converge;
- the existing field-receipt bridge validates `sync.two_device` and physical discovery evidence.

Same-host processes, VMs/containers without a separate physical witness, remote desktop, CI, or Drive/cloud copying do not self-certify this gate.
