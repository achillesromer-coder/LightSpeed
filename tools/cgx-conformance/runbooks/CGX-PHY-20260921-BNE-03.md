## Required scenarios

1. `baseline-mutual-auth` — secure peer identity and authority/capability validation in both directions.
2. `network-discovery` — physical LAN discovery/route observation plus secure identity verification.
3. `disconnect-no-canonical-mutation` — peer disappearance alone cannot mutate canonical roots.
4. `divergent-offline-edits` — independent non-overlapping edits on disconnected replicas.
5. `reconnect-semantic-convergence` — reconnect through the existing secure sync and semantic merge paths.
6. `stale-peer-rejection` — stale state/lease/evidence cannot overwrite a newer descendant.
7. `revoked-peer-rejection` — revoked or unauthorised peer is rejected.
8. `interrupted-transfer-resume` — partial session transfer resumes; incomplete/tampered chunks never become canonical.
9. `authority-handover-continuity` — explicit signed TRACK continuity/handover.
10. `final-root-convergence` — independent verifier PASS with equal final content and DBR roots.

## Evidence

Each node should retain machine-readable evidence for preflight, mutual auth, discovery, disconnect, offline divergence, reconnect merge, stale/revoked rejection, transfer resume, authority handover, final verification and its final signed `node-receipt.json`.

Screenshots/logs are supporting evidence only; hash and reference them rather than treating prose as authority.

## Acceptance

The physical gate may close only when:
- two genuinely distinct physical devices participated;
- relevant test-plane traffic used direct non-loopback device-to-device transport;
- node receipts use distinct device IDs, keys and endpoints and validate independently;
- every required scenario has machine evidence;
- final canonical roots converge;
- the existing field-receipt bridge validates `sync.two_device` and physical discovery evidence.

Same-host processes, VMs/containers without a separate physical witness, remote desktop, CI, or Drive/cloud copying do not self-certify this gate.
